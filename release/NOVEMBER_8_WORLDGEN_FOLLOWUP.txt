November 8 world-generation follow-up

Player-visible changes
- Terracotta region width reduced by 40%, from 288 to 172.8 nominal blocks.
- Mooshrooms, stalkers, hoglins and striders receive more frequent natural-spawn attempts and larger independent population budgets. Mushroom/Nether cave caps are 40 each; Deep Dark remains half of those at 20. Normal player-distance, habitat and collision rules still apply.
- Sculk sensor/shrieker patch opportunities tripled, preserving compact patches and their distribution along corridors.
- Desert cave entrances accumulate variable spiral rotation as they ascend, with varied opening positions and headings. Lower tunnels retain their layout. Dry-mouth detection and the interpolated tunnel junction were corrected.
- Snowy habitat selection follows the same X/Z column above and below the surface, with Mushroom Caves retained in their underground band. Snowy forest begins at the terrain profile's Y70 contour. Sealed noise caves with fewer than 20 supported floor tiles are filled without trimming larger caves or authored rooms.
- New orc settlements use one curved 90-by-78 palisade with native logs, posts, skulls and leaf bushes. Six detailed native-style buildings, nine chests, wheat/carrot plots and exactly two livestock pens are inside it. The former watchtower, bone pit and pond bones are absent. Saved earlier settlements retain compatibility.
- Skeleton and zombie horses burn in exposed sunlight; camel husks are excluded.
- Spider caves have a solid steep entrance hill and a covered horizontal mouth. Both longer descending branches have two cave pockets each and lead to a deeper boss room. The former hollow dome is gone.
- Mulberry items, recipes and natural placement are disabled. Isolated mangrove mounds are removed; rice, sugarcane and the former mound plants/fauna use the connected shore.
- The new dry mangrove strip is about 23.916618 blocks wide. Existing island ground rises two blocks while rainforest X/Z positions stay fixed. The coral reef shifts outward only about 11.748006 blocks, the old Y63 band's width, leaving a narrower water band. Rainforest hostile altitude zones also rise two blocks to preserve their habitats.
- Ten cotton/pitcher textures use direct green-pixel recoloring. Their shapes, transparency, white cotton, purple/brown parts and mature pitcher colors are preserved.
- Boss placement is tuned using accepted-site Euclidean neighbor measurements. Candidate spacing alone is not treated as the resulting distance; terrain and seed variation remain.
- Removed the four isolated roof-top blocks from Wither caves while keeping the roofs sealed.
- Added five Spider, three Wither and two Yeti loot chests, with matching loot tables and supported placement after native decoration.
