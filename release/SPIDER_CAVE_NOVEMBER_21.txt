SPIDER CAVE — NOVEMBER 21, 2026

Applies to newly generated spider caves in the Downloads project. Existing saved
caves retain their original layout and resident flags.

The cave has six themed chambers: slime habitat, azalea garden, huge-mushroom
grove, Arthropleura farming larder, silk nursery, and queen chamber. The two
branches descend to the queen 34 blocks below the covered entrance. A new cross
gallery links the upper chambers. Total floor area is 4,256 blocks, up from
2,657; it remains smaller than both the historical and current orc camp.

Each cave starts with exactly 20 spiders, 20 cave spiders, 8 bogged, 10 small
slimes, 10 slime beetles, one spider queen, and 4 adult Arthropleura in two lowered
pits. The 68 regular monsters are below the requested maximum of 96. Slimes and
beetles inhabit both their dedicated room and other chambers. Small slimes do
not split. Random spider jockey passengers are removed. Saved placement flags
cover every resident, including indices above 64, without replenishment on reload.
Arthropleura offspring, including twins, inherit cave membership so the farming
pits remain usable across breeding and reloads.

Mature glowing cave vines, inset ochre/verdant/pearlescent frog lights, and spore
blossoms decorate the ceiling. Floors and room edges include slime blocks,
moss, grass, ordinary ferns, red/brown mushrooms, two huge mushrooms,
azalea and flowering azalea shrubs, one azalea tree, bone blocks, white wool cocoons, and cobwebs.
Vanilla azalea trees use oak logs beneath an exclusively azalea leaf canopy.
The entrance, central paths, resident positions and chest approaches stay clear.
Six loot chests face inward against room walls. No swamp water, drowned, wild
crops, or non-azalea/non-mushroom trees were added.

Optional future additions: a small calcite fossil gallery with bone ribs and
tuff accents; a flowering-azalea alcove with decorative bee nests and honeycomb.
These are recommendations, not additional rooms in this revision.

Validation commands:
.\gradlew.bat -I scripts/spider-cave-qa.init.gradle --max-workers=2 build
.\gradlew.bat -I scripts/spider-cave-qa.init.gradle --max-workers=2 '-PspiderRun=check' runGameTestServer

QA runs use disposable build/spider-cave-qa directories, immutable runtime
copies, two active processors, and modest heaps. Installed configs, saves and
options are not used by the test runtime.
