# November 1 mob, terrain, and vegetation revision

This supersedes the older terrain and wild-crop descriptions in `DIMENSIONS.md`. Terrain, structures, and natural vegetation changes take effect in newly generated chunks.

## Launching with less memory

Double-click `Launch Minecraft.cmd`, or run `powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/launch-client.ps1` from the project folder. This prepares the current mod and assets, waits for Gradle to finish, and launches Java directly using the normal `run/` saves and settings. It rejects a second launch while the project client is running.

The launcher uses a 1 GB initial / 6 GB maximum Java heap and four JVM-visible processors. Native allocations and graphics buffers add to the process total; the heap limit is not a total-process memory limit. Concurrent periodic G1 collections are enabled to allow unused heap to be reclaimed during idle periods. The ordinary Gradle client also now has the explicit heap/processor limits, while the direct launcher additionally removes Gradle's memory overhead during play.

Launch diagnostics are saved under `build/standalone-client/`; GC events are in `run/logs/standalone-client-gc.log`. `-PrepareOnly` prepares without starting the game, and `-SkipPrepare` starts the last prepared snapshot without rebuilding.

The November 1 freeze report showed roughly 9.6 GB for Minecraft and 1.8 GB for its Gradle processes. Windows had no recorded display reset or memory-exhaustion event around the incident, so the full cause was not established. The logs also exposed duplicate biome-modifier registrations introduced by the Orcz suppression configuration. Those ten overrides now use separate empty spawn modifiers, verified through registry identity and repeated-decoding tests; they still add no unwanted spawns or features.

Standalone verification reached the ordinary menu with one Java process and no Gradle process. JVM inspection confirmed the 6 GB heap maximum, and the short idle sample measured about 7 GB process working set with over 9 GB of system RAM available. The window responded normally. This was a menu test, not a sustained gameplay benchmark. The test was closed normally afterward, leaving no Java processes and about 16.6 GB available. `build`, `runData`, launcher preparation, duplicate-launch rejection, and all nine targeted mob/cache regression tests passed.

Mangrove, coral reef, mushroom caves, and crimson forest have no natural monsters. These restrictions do not remove monsters that walk in. Endermen and wardens reject mutually forbidden targets before anger sounds, listening/roaring, or angry animation flags begin. Existing passive livestock, including the pack's passive hoglins, retain their fauna rules.

- `aoemod:spider_cave`: centered on the cherry grove of about one in twelve tropical islands. A moss dome has continuous side walls, one diagonal entrance, mature glowing cave-vine tips, interior grass/mushrooms, a moss roof supporting regular tropical vegetation, and one spider queen with spider/cave-spider support.
- `aoemod:illager_mansion`: rare vanilla woodland-mansion rooms and residents, plus one persistent illager lord in an inhabited room.
- `aoemod:yeti_cave`: the real Twilight Forest Yeti Cave component and Alpha Yeti spawner, surrounded by a snowy hill and supported by yetis and breezes.
- `aoemod:wither_cave`: a rare obsidian arena with magma cubes and wither skeletons. Obsidian is wither-immune; withers target only players, villagers, and piglins.
- `aoemod:orc_camp`: a rare open savanna camp with a 200-health large orc boss and a boss bar.

Bosses do not enter ordinary spawn lotteries. Redcaps cannot plant/light TNT; upper goblin knights walk alone, have no red back spine, retain their armor, and lose four armor points. Creakings use native gaze, heart, and resin behavior with the underground day/night clock. Rubber-belly gliders use the ambient-fish despawn range. Natural mob collision checks reject powder cane, and camels/rabbits require ordinary sand.

Orcs come from the pinned Forge 1.20.1 [Orcz 0.87 release](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/orcz/files/4816086), whose internal mod version is 0.82. `scripts/bootstrap-orcz.ps1` verifies its SHA256. Unrequested Orcz natural spawns and structures are disabled.

## Terrain

- Savanna uses the upper-desert contour at four times X/Z frequency with dry relief divided by 32, giving one-eighth the original upper-desert world-space slope. Underwater slope doubles. Shorter cycles produce closer lakes and lower peaks.
- Desert sea level is 54 with a solid floor at 49, retaining at most four water blocks. Sandy grade is divided by three; its noise band remains approximately the old width. Red sand starts where steepness increases and spans 2.5 times its former noise-space width. Sampling 2,359,296 columns across four seeds gives 49.9752% of the previous water coverage.
- Desert ordinary caves have one surface connection on a consistent side of the existing tunnel. Lake-biome carving is suppressed only at Y >= 50. High exits extend farther at a fixed climb grade, and dry-land entrances use air.
- Snowy terrain has a flat deep lake bed, one-quarter underwater bank slope, a forest grade reduced by 2.5, and an even gentler forest shoreline. The mountain slope rises into a smooth shoulder and flat snowy plateau. Lake and forest grass colors match.
- Snowy independent ravines/carvers and aquifer liquids/barriers are removed. Cheese layers retain their noise scales and connect through interruptions. Wider shared pillars remain solid through the cave passes; smaller outlets begin in underground caves and avoid lake columns.
- Deep-dark/Nether passage margins are 50% longer without doubling the walking tunnel. They share pillar geometry. Seeded pillars that obstruct bridges are shifted to a nearby clear position, retaining their random placement and count. Basalt has a continuous central lava river, dry banks on both sides, transverse bridges connected to the bottom stair landings, maximum depth eight, and three blocks below its deepest lava including bedrock. Deep-dark stair and tunnel treads retain sculk even where the deepslate backing is exposed.

## Vegetation and materials

- Tree connectors accept existing horizontal diagonal routes, add the fewest logs, and favor foliage or a support beneath the upper log.
- Plains mushroom searches use 16 stratified columns and a one-third lottery. Plains surface rules also skip an unused lake classification; seeded classifier copies share a bounded cache and reuse simultaneous requests for the same column. Desert cave mushrooms have one opportunity in four chunks, selecting either color and placing five mushrooms within 6-by-6.
- Dappled native poplars use spaced candidates at the previous total tree budget and retain their native size weights. Spruce is removed. Red shrubs are half as common.
- Snowy mineral blobs use smooth basalt. Basalt rejects glowstone clusters touching floors/ledges while retaining buried patches. Powder cane has one tree in two thirds of suitable basalt land chunks. Mature top blocks drop one bullet pepper in addition to the replantable cane; lower or immature blocks only drop cane.
- Sculk sensor/shrieker patches use 10-by-10 footprints and five plants, with one opportunity in twenty chunks (five times rarer). Vein patches are five times larger at the old frequency.
- Sweet and blue berries use one patch per 120-by-120 tile; pumpkins use two. Soul berry plants and items are removed. Soul sand valleys instead have five nether wart within 6-by-6 once per suitable 120-by-120 tile. Wild soul beet and dragon fruit generation is removed; their seeds come from soul weeds and desert dry grass respectively.
- Eyeblossom patches have half the per-chunk opportunity of twisting vines, with five flowers within 6-by-6. Lava plants are evenly split between crimson and warped, with 80% short seagrass (previously 40%). Basalt quartz/gold exposed-ore discard is 75%, while other Nether biomes retain 100%.
